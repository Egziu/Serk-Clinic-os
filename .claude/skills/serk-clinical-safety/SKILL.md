# serk-clinical-safety

Maintain a hazard register for wrong patient/tooth/site, allergies, wrong units, pregnancy dating, pediatric reference ranges, missed critical results, duplicate dispensing and unsigned/signed confusion. Explain what clinical evidence is missing, identify testing scenarios and classify each feature Pending Clinician Review until real reviewer approval. Do not endorse a clinical calculation based only on AI interpretation.

When invoked, leave: (1) precise findings/implementation, (2) commands that actually ran, (3) tests and evidence paths, (4) high-risk unresolved gaps, (5) next action.
