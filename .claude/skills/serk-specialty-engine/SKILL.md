# serk-specialty-engine

Extend shared Encounter via typed specialty records and versioned clinical forms. Dental: FDI primary/adult tooth/surface and accessible SVG. Dermatology: consent-controlled photos, lesion laterality, original/derivative provenance. OB/GYN: confidential history, pregnancy episodes and clinician-reviewed dating. Pediatrics: guardian access and approved growth references. Maintain separate test suites and clinical-review blockers. Do not invent dosing protocols.

When invoked, leave: (1) precise findings/implementation, (2) commands that actually ran, (3) tests and evidence paths, (4) high-risk unresolved gaps, (5) next action.
