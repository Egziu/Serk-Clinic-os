# Adding staff and customising roles (clinic owner)

Who: anyone with **Manage staff and roles** (`staff.manage`) — the Owner by default.
Where: **Team & roles** in the left menu (or press Ctrl/⌘+K and type "staff").

## Add a person
1. Team & roles → **Manage people** → **+ New person**.
2. Enter full name and a username (lowercase, e.g. `liya.dentist`). A strong temporary password is generated — give it to the
   person privately (in person or by phone, never in a group chat).
3. Choose **Works at** (branch) and tick one or more **roles** (e.g. Dentist, Receptionist, Cashier, or your custom role).
4. **Create person.** At first sign-in they must choose their own password; until then they can do nothing else.
5. For dentists and clinicians: open the person → **Practitioner profile** → title, specialty, licence number and expiry.
   Without a valid licence they cannot sign notes or chart teeth.

## Customise a role
1. Team & roles → **Custom roles** → **Clone** a built-in role (or **+ New custom role**).
2. Name it (e.g. "Dental assistant") and tick the permissions. Each permission shows how sensitive it is
   (Standard, Patient data, Clinical, Administrative). Built-in roles cannot be edited — they are the tested safety baseline.
3. Save, then give the role to people as above.

## Fine-tune one person
Open the person → **Edit** their access: add or remove single permissions on top of their roles, set start/end dates
(e.g. a locum dentist for two weeks), and write a reason. The live preview shows exactly what they will be able to do and
warns about risky combinations (e.g. clinical + administrative in one account).

## Other actions
Deactivate (ends all their sessions at once), reset password (temporary), unlock after failed sign-ins, end sessions.
Every change is recorded in the change log with who, when, before/after and reason.

## Limits (for safety)
- You cannot change **your own** access or password here — ask another owner or the system administrator.
- People who also work for **another organisation**, and system super administrators, are managed by the system administrator.
- You only see and change your own organisation's branches, people and roles.
